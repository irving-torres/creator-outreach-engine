# Lead Scoring Rubric

Every candidate brand is scored against these criteria before any draft is written.

## Vetting (pass/fail)

- Real company with real products and a legitimate online presence.
- No dropshipped, counterfeit, or scam signals (fake followers, AI-generated spokespeople, no verifiable address or product).
- No conflicts with standing exclusions (alcohol, gambling, previously vetoed brands).

Fail any of these: flagged "suspected scam, no draft". No outreach is written, ever.

## Fit check (scored)

1. **Budget plausibility:** would this brand plausibly pay $1,000+ for one sponsored post or video? Typical $60-150 UGC offers are below the bar.
2. **Lane fit:** does it fit at least one content lane (here: San Diego lifestyle, tech and mobility, sports)?
3. **Audience overlap:** does the brand's customer look like the creator's audience?
4. **Proof angle:** is there a natural, specific reason this brand fits (local presence, product already used, event tie-in)?

Brands need a plausible yes on budget and lane fit to survive. The proof angle is what makes the draft write itself.

## Contact quality

- Verified partnerships, influencer, PR, or media email from an official source: full marks.
- Contact form or LinkedIn route only: noted as alternate route.
- No contact found and no plausible route: parked, not dropped.

## Cadence

Re-run monthly. Skip brands already contacted unless a new angle or contact appears.
